# Urban Revival (NASA SEES 2025)

Data and analysis from the Urban Revival team at NASA SEES (STEM Enhancement in Earth Science), hosted by the UT Austin Center for Space Research.

We used satellite remote sensing data and machine learning (Random Forest and XGBoost) to study urban greenery loss and how it relates to gentrification and disinvestment. The project involved labeling 222 land-cover points to train and validate our models.

Our work was presented at NASA's Virtual Science Symposium (IVSS) and accepted to the American Geophysical Union (AGU) 2025 Fall Meeting.
